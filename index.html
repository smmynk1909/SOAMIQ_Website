About the lab

Research that ships.

We sit at the intersection of research and product. Our team designs, trains, and deploys AI systems that are reliable, observable, and built to scale — not demos that break in production.

Every engagement starts with the problem, not the model. We pick the simplest approach that works, instrument it end-to-end, and iterate with real-world feedback.

  • Pragmatic, problem-first engineering
  • Transparent, measurable outcomes
  • Production hardening from day one
